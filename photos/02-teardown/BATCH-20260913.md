# 2026-09-13 final disassembly photo batch

This batch contains 119 photographs captured during and immediately after the printer teardown.

## Scope

- IDs: `TD-20260913-041` through `TD-20260913-159`
- Destination: `photos/02-teardown/originals/`
- Images are preserved byte-for-byte from the supplied archive. Only filenames were changed.
- `inventory-20260913.csv` maps each renamed file to its original camera filename.
- `SHA256SUMS-20260913.txt` provides SHA-256 hashes for integrity verification.

The batch is not a complete step-by-step photographic record of the teardown. It primarily documents the final disassembled state and recovered components.

Some printed parts show visible damage. Damage timing must remain unclassified unless independently confirmed as pre-existing or caused during teardown.
