## Evidence distinctions

- The original archaeology baseline comprises 80 photographs. The archive now also contains nine later spare-box photographs, `ARQ-20260915-081`–`089`. Those nine were metadata-sanitized before publication despite their legacy `originals/` location; their [source note](../../00-archaeology/06-motion-electronics-spares.md) records this distinction.
- Teardown has 159 preserved originals and 69 cleaned component-inventory derivatives. See the [processing policy](../../../photos/02-teardown/derived/component-inventory/README.md).
- Reassembly has 116 sanitized evidence photographs across four batches. The [16 September batch](../../../photos/03-reassembly/BATCH-20260916.md) covers Stages B–G; the latest [21 September batch](../../../photos/03-reassembly/BATCH-20260921.md) adds 14 Stage H geared-extruder photographs. All repository JPEG hashes are recorded; the latest batch explains its unavailable pre-encoding source-pixel hashes. Photo coverage alone does not establish a passed mechanical or electrical test.
- [The old as-found index](../../../photos/00-as-found/INDEX.md) is historical planning material superseded by the ARQ archive. Do not create competing `AF` copies.

The [archaeology archive summary](../../../photos/00-archaeology/README.md) now distinguishes the 80-image historical baseline from the nine later sanitized photographs, matching the catalogue and spare-box record. The reconciliation is recorded as resolved KB-001 in [Open issues](../project/Open-Issues.md#resolved-items); all image files and permanent IDs are unchanged.

## Adding or interpreting evidence

Follow the [method and provenance rules](../../00-archaeology/03-method-and-provenance.md). Preserve existing IDs and original files. Keep crops, annotations and publication versions distinct from originals, recording their source and processing. Update the owning archive index, manifest and affected project notes together. Generated diagrams belong in [Illustrations](Illustrations.md), not among photographs presented as physical evidence.
