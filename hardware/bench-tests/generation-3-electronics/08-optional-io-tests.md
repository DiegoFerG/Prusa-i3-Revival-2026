# 08 — Optional low-risk I/O tests

These tests broaden warranty coverage but are not required to establish basic operation of the purchased controller, drivers and toolboard.

Possible low-risk checks:

- one known-good 100K NTC on a documented Manta thermistor input;
- one known-voltage low-power fan on a correctly jumpered Manta fan output;
- one known-good 100K NTC on the documented EBB36 external thermistor input.

Confirm physically plausible readings and smooth response to gentle warming; for a fan, test progressively at low, medium and full duty, then off.

## Do not power-test yet

Do not energise the hotend heater, heated bed, external bed MOSFET or other high-current loads. Those belong to the final electrical build with the correct fuses, wire gauges, connectors, grounding and independent thermal protection already designed.
