# 01 — Receiving inspection

Before power-up, record the actual hardware that arrived.

## Required photographs

Capture clear images of:

- outer packaging
- supplier/product labels
- Manta front and back
- exact Manta PCB revision markings
- all six TMC2209 modules, both sides
- revision markings on each TMC module
- EBB36 Gen2 front and back
- EBB36 Gen2 adapter/protection board
- included cables and accessories
- any impact mark, bent pin, suspect solder joint or shipping damage

Temporarily identify the six plug-in drivers as TMC-A through TMC-F. Do not place permanent labels over components, heatsinks or test points.

## Visual rejection checks

Look for bent pins, distorted connectors, loose metal debris, obviously poor or bridged solder joints, displaced components, damaged fuses, struck capacitors, moisture/corrosion or abnormal odour.

If a component has significant physical damage, **do not energise it**. Record the condition first.
